06 / THE ARTISTCHICAGO, ILLINOIS / SINCE 2013

NEVER
STOOD STILL.

It started as Lucki Eck$. Then the sound kept changing, the records kept accumulating, and a singular voice became impossible to ignore.

LUCKI emerged from Chicago's experimental rap underground with Alternative Trap in 2013. The hazy atmosphere and off-center rhythmic choices of his early projects established an independent creative identity.

From the Freewave releases and Watch My Back to Days B4 III, Almost There and WAKE UP LUCKI, each chapter brought a different texture to an unmistakable voice.

The albums FLAWLESS LIKE ME, s*x m*ney dr*gs and GEMINI! marked further growth in reach and scale. In 2026, Last Laugh (archive tape) returned the focus to recordings and moments longtime listeners remembered.
